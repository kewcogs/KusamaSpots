Yayoi Kusama 1929 - 2026

This kinetic image is my simple homage to Yayoi Kusama who died on the 14th August 2026, inspired by my visit to her 2012 exhibition at Tate Modern.

The spots you see are connected through a mesh of invisible elastic. Drag one from its resting position and it will twang back, creating a disturbance in its neighbours which eventually propagates over the entire mesh.

Bigger spots are heavier and more sluggish in their movements, but they also have a bigger effect on those around.

Your inner child will be excited to drag a spot to a far corner and marvel at the chaos as it catapults across the screen. The more contemplative you may find more subtle disturbances restful, even hypnotic.
